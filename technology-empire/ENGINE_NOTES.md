# Engine design notes (shared contract for all engine modules)

Time: 1 tick = 1 day, 30-day months, 360-day years. `s.day % 30 === 0` → month end. Orchestrator: `src/engine/sim.ts` (do not change call order without coordinating).
Determinism: ONLY use `src/engine/rng.ts` with `s` (never Math.random / Date). Engine must be pure TS (no DOM, no React).
Accounting: cash-basis. Every cash change of a company must be mirrored in its `ledger` (types.Ledger): revenue, cogsMaterials, salaries, managerSalaries, energy, maintenance, logistics, marketing, rnd, infra, other, interest, tax, depreciation(non-cash), capex(not an expense), dividends. Month-end (finance) converts ledger → MonthlyReport and resets ledger. `netIncome = revenue − (all expense fields incl. depreciation)` (capex & dividends excluded).
Commodity trade: ALWAYS use `src/engine/sim/trade.ts` (buyFromMarket, sellToMarket, buyInternal, acquireGood). They update market `boughtToday/soldToday` and ledgers.
Shared helpers: `src/engine/util.ts` (clamp, countryCosts, techEffects, companyEfficiency, requiredProductionWorkers, adminRequired, SALARY_MULT, makeManager, addNews, newCompany), `src/engine/preview.ts` (previewProduct, frontierPerf, facilityBuildCost, facilityUpgradeCost, marketOpenCost, loanOffer, researchRate), `src/engine/selectors.ts`. Data: `src/engine/data/defs.ts`, `src/engine/data/countries.ts`.

## Economics (target magnitudes)
- Start: player ~12M cash (normal). Founding costs in SECTORS. A single assembly plant (120 units/day L1) with a decent GPU should earn ~$0.3–1M/month once it has markets; competition and limited market presence keep it bounded.
- Countries: `marketWeight` sums to 1. Consumer demand of a category in country = `baseDemand × macro.demandIndex × (1+annualGrowth)^(day/360) × seasonality(±6%, sin over year) × marketWeight`. A company can only sell in countries listed in `company.markets`.
- Consumer product attractiveness (per product): `relPerf = performance / frontierPerf(cat)`; `fair = refPrice × relPerf^0.9 × (0.85 + quality/333)`; `A = relPerf^perfWeight × (0.6+quality/250) × (0.6+brand/125) × (0.7+reliability/333) × (1 + 0.35·ln(1 + marketing/(refPrice·20))) × (price/fair)^(−elasticity)`. Outside option A0 = 0.35. Share in country = A/(ΣA + A0) over products available there (active & stock>0 & company present). Sales limited by stock (scale proportionally when demand > stock).
- Commodities: npc demand `= npcVolume × demandIndex × (p/base·priceLevel)^−0.8`, npc supply `= npcVolume × supplyShock × (p/base·priceLevel)^0.6`. Daily `p *= exp(clamp(0.08 × (D_total − S_total)/npcVolume, −0.04, 0.04) + volatility·gauss)` with D_total = npcDemand + boughtToday, S_total = npcSupply + soldToday; then reset today counters. Keep history (180).
- Mine output/day = `FACILITIES.mine.capacity(=9000 USD-value) / GOODS[g].basePrice × level × purity × richness × utilization × efficiency × techEffects.extraction`; reserve decreases; reserve 0 → no output. Initial reserve ≈ 8 years at L1.
- Processing: outputs `FACILITIES[type].capacity × level × util × eff` units of `GOODS[out].recipe` inputs; defect = `baseDefect × (1.5 − condition/200) / techEff.efficiency × (1 − COO·0.003)`.
- Manufacturing (assemblyPlant, output = productId): inputs = product.bom per unit; produced units go to `productStock[productId]`.
- Facility daily costs: energy `energyMWh × level × utilEff × countryCosts.elec`, maintenance `maintenance × level × facility.maintenance`. Condition `+= 0.06·maint − 0.08·util − 0.01` (clamp 0–100). Breakdown chance/day `0.0004 × (1 + (100−condition)/20)` → downDays 5–20 + news (important for player).
- Salaries daily = Σdept employees × countryCosts(HQ).wageMonthly × SALARY_MULT[dept] / 30 (production workers paid at facility countries weighted by their workers); managers salary/30 → managerSalaries.
- Cloud: service demand = `baseDemand × (1+growth)^(years) × demandIndex`; offers logit `A = (price/refPrice)^−elasticity × (0.6+brand/125) × (0.7+avgCondition/333)·(1+0.35ln(1+mkt/(refPrice·2000)))`, A0 = 0.3; sold ≤ capacity (Σ dcCapacity of DCs with that service, × condition/downtime). Energy per unit = energyPerUnit × elec (× (1−dcEfficiency)).
- Platforms: see PLATFORMS defs. signups = `users × viral × (0.5+engagement) × (1 − catUsers/addressable) × trend × presence + marketing/CAC($2.5)`, capped by spare infraCapacity; churn = `users × baseChurn × (1 + max(0,(60−satisfaction))/30)`. DAU = users × dauRatio × (0.6+engagement). Ads revenue = `DAU × impressionsPerDau × adLoad × cpm/1000`; premium users = `users × premiumRate × (refPremiumPrice/premiumPrice)^1.3`; subs revenue/day = premiumUsers × premiumPrice/30. Infra cost = DAU × infraPerDau (cheaper by 20% if holding owns a cloud company — revenue goes to it); moderation = users × modPerUser × moderation × (1−techCostReduction). Satisfaction drifts to `70 + features·1.5 − adLoad·40 − overload·50 − (1−moderation)·15 + (moderation−1)·5`; trust drifts with moderation. features += devSpend / (40000 × (1+features)) per day. engagement = clamp(0.25 + features·0.015 + techEffects.engagement, 0, 0.95). New platform: 2,000 users, infraCapacity 50,000.
- Stock valuation daily: `annualProfit = profit30×12`, growth g from reports (revenue now vs 3 months ago, clamp −0.5..1); PE = clamp(14 + 30g, 5, 45)·(0.7 + 0.3·demandIndex); value = max(bookEquity×0.8, annualProfit×PE + bookEquity×0.4, revenue30×12×0.6×(1+g)) − 0.3·debt; target = max(0.01, value/shares); `price *= exp(0.04·ln(target/price) + 0.012·gauss)`; keep priceHistory (120). Private companies get the same valuation (used for net worth).
- Loans: annuity monthly payment; interest part → ledger.interest; principal reduces balance (not expense).
- Tax monthly on positive pre-tax income at HQ countryCosts.tax × (1 − CFO·0.002).
- Bankruptcy: month-end cash < 0 → AI takes emergency loan if loanOffer allows, else distressMonths++; reset to 0 when cash ≥ 0. distressMonths ≥ 3 → bankrupt=true (facilities liquidated at 30% book, debt written off, products deactivated, shareholders lose value, important news). Player holding cash < 0 is not allowed by actions.
- AI holdings: NEXORA (GPU/CPU), SILICORE (chips), MEMTRON (RAM/SSD + memory), CLOUDIX (cloud), VISTAGRAM (social), NEURALIS (AI software), VOLTERRA (mobile/battery, lithium), DATANEX (data center + servers), plus TERRAMIN (mining). AI companies are public: holding owns 45%, 55% free float. Player companies are private (100%) until IPO.
- News: use addNews; keep random events rare (~1–2/month, min 10 days gap) and meaningful.
